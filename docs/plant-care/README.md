# Plant & Leaf Diseases Quick Guide

For chatbot use: concise answers to common user questions about plant health, symptoms, and care steps.

## General best practices
- Use clear photos: natural light, single leaf filling most of the frame, steady shot.
- Water at soil level; avoid wetting leaves. Improve airflow and avoid overcrowding.
- Remove heavily infected leaves; sterilize tools between cuts.
- Test treatments on a small area first. Follow product labels and local regulations.
- Isolate sick plants from healthy ones when possible.

## Healthy vs. diseased indicators
- Healthy: firm green leaves, no spots, no wilting, normal growth, no sticky residue or webbing.
- Diseased/stressed: yellowing, browning, dark water-soaked spots, fuzzy mold, powdery coating, distorted leaves, webbing or tiny moving dots (mites), stunted growth.

## Common diseases and care tips
- **Bacterial spot**: small water-soaked spots that turn brown/black. Remove affected leaves; avoid overhead watering; improve airflow. Copper-based treatments may help; follow label.
- **Early blight**: brown concentric rings on older leaves. Remove infected leaves; improve airflow; rotate crops; use labeled fungicide if needed.
- **Late blight**: dark, fast-spreading lesions with pale edges; can affect stems and fruit. Remove and destroy infected plants; avoid overhead watering; use labeled fungicide promptly.
- **Leaf mold**: yellow patches on top, olive/brown fuzz underneath. Improve ventilation; reduce humidity; remove infected leaves; fungicide if severe.
- **Powdery mildew**: white powdery coating. Improve airflow, avoid shade and crowding; remove worst leaves; use sulfur or bicarbonate-based sprays per label.
- **Septoria leaf spot**: small tan/gray spots with dark borders. Remove infected leaves; water at soil level; improve airflow; rotate crops; fungicide if persistent.
- **Mosaic virus**: mottled/yellow mosaic patterns, distorted leaves. No cure; remove infected plants; control pests that spread virus; avoid reusing contaminated tools.
- **Yellow leaf curl virus**: curling, yellowing, stunted growth. No cure; remove infected plants; control whiteflies; use resistant varieties if available.
- **Spider mites** (pest): tiny dots, stippling, fine webbing. Rinse leaves; increase humidity; use insecticidal soap/miticide as labeled; isolate plant.

## Prevention tips to share with users
- Buy healthy starts; quarantine new plants for a short period.
- Clean pots/tools; use well-draining soil; avoid waterlogged roots.
- Rotate crops and avoid planting the same family in the same spot every season.
- Support good airflow: proper spacing, pruning crowded foliage.
- Monitor weekly for early signs; act quickly when symptoms appear.

## FAQ prompts the chatbot can answer
- How do I take a good photo for analysis?
- What do common symptoms (spots, powder, webbing, yellowing, mosaic patterns) mean?
- How should I water and space plants to reduce disease?
- What immediate steps should I take if the AI says "Diseased"?
- Are the recommendations guaranteed? (No—guidance only; verify locally.)
- Can I keep using the plant? (Remove badly infected tissue; monitor; replace if severe or viral.)
- How are my images used? (To analyze and display your results/history; no other purpose stated.)
